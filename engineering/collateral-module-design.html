Journey notes

1 · Holdings discovery

Fetch via MFCentral / CAMS / KFintech APIs (Phase 3) or statement upload (Phase 2). Eligible value = market value × LTV cap per scheme category — the policy engine drives the math, not the UI.

2 · OTP pledge

Lien marking is authorised by the investor via RTA OTP. For demat shares the equivalent step is NSDL/CDSL pledge authorisation. Failures fall back to ops-assisted manual lien.

3 · Live limit + headroom

Post-pledge, borrower sees LTV headroom continuously (P1 story #9). Margin call notices reuse the same notification templates as the ops console (MC-0038 flow).

States to design later

KYC mismatch, folio under existing lien, partial pledge rejection by RTA, NAV feed delay banner, invocation notice.
